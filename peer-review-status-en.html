Statement

This is an open preprint posted online: it has DOIs, is citable and verifiable, but it has not been peer reviewed, is not a journal article, and represents no publisher or scholarly consensus. Every archive, submission-console page and Q&A page keeps this wording.

What has been completed is open self-archiving and index registration: immutable copies on Zenodo, Figshare, OSF and GitHub, DataCite registration, OpenAIRE inclusion, and redundancy across independent document hosts. The viXra submission was made by the author; its receipt says the request was received and will be checked, with no identifier or public link yet. No response within two days means it was not accepted, and viXra asks authors not to resubmit.

Cite it as a preprint: Didgeridoo (2026), The Labor Theory of Value as a Historical Category: The Contemporary Crisis of Value, CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24269665 (concept DOI) or a specific version DOI. Do not describe it as published in a journal or as peer reviewed.

When using this work in teaching, reviews or further research, state both its theoretical character and its unreviewed status. If you cite a guide or Q&A page, note that those are explanatory material, not the paper itself.
