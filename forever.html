Design principle

Any single surviving copy must be sufficient to reconstruct the entire work. Copies are spread across independent institutions, jurisdictions and protocols (DOI repositories, git hosting, UNESCO source archive, web time-capsule, global CDNs, scholarly indices and offline plain-text bundles).

4 · Honest boundary

No digital system can guarantee persistence beyond the existence of the institutions and readers maintaining it. This manifest maximises redundancy, it does not promise perpetuity.

All public copies use the author name Didgeridoo only. The deprecated Figshare record 35018683 v1/v2 still carries an early attribution and is under an official take-down request — do not share it. This is an open preprint with DOIs, not a peer-reviewed journal publication.
