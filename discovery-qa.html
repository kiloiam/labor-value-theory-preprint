中文问答 / English Q&A

劳动价值论是什么？
What is the labor theory of value?

劳动价值论认为商品的价值由生产它所耗费的社会必要劳动时间决定，是古典政治经济学与马克思政治经济学批判的核心范畴。它通常以雇佣劳动与商品交换的普遍化为前提。本站收录的开放预印本《劳动价值论是历史性范畴：当代价值危机》正是从这一前提出发讨论该理论的界限。

English answer

The labor theory of value holds that the value of a commodity is determined by the socially necessary labour time required to produce it. It is central to classical political economy and Marx's critique, and normally presupposes wage labour and generalized commodity exchange. The open preprint hosted here, "The Labor Theory of Value as a Historical Category", examines precisely those historical premises.

劳动价值论是历史性范畴吗？
Is the labor theory of value a historically specific category?

这是该预印本的核心论点：劳动价值论不是适用于一切社会形态的普遍经济原理，而是以雇佣劳动、社会必要劳动时间和商品交换普遍化为前提的历史性理论。作者认为，其失效不是理论被证伪，而是商品社会走到了历史尽头。该文为网络预发布预印本，未经同行评审。

English answer

That is the central claim of the preprint: the LTV is not a universal principle but a historically specific theory premised on wage labour, socially necessary labour time and generalized commodity exchange. Its obsolescence, the author argues, does not refute the theory but signals the historical endpoint of commodity society. This is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed.

自动化时代劳动价值论还成立吗？
Does the labor theory of value still hold in the age of automation?

该预印本构建包含自动化技术资本的价值修正模型，论证当自动化系统在原则上能脱离人类活劳动独立承担生产职能时，可变资本被系统性挤出，剩余价值生产机制趋于枯竭，价值范畴与商品形式发生根本性瓦解。这是作者提出的待讨论论点，不是学界定论，全文开放可查。

English answer

The preprint builds a revised value model incorporating automated technological capital and argues that, once automated systems can in principle perform production independently of living labour, variable capital is expelled and surplus-value production tends towards exhaustion. This is the author's argument open for discussion, not a settled scholarly consensus; the full text is freely available.

劳动价值论成立的历史前提有哪些？
What are the historical premises of the labor theory of value?

按该预印本的梳理，主要包括：雇佣劳动的普遍化、以社会必要劳动时间作为价值尺度、以及商品交换的普遍化。文章把三者视为历史条件而非自然事实，并据此讨论当条件改变时范畴的界限。

English answer

According to the preprint: the generalization of wage labour, socially necessary labour time as the measure of value, and the universalization of commodity exchange. The paper treats these as historical conditions rather than natural facts, and asks what happens to the category when they change.

「维稳成本 P」是什么意思？
What does the 'cost of social stabilization P' mean?

这是该预印本提出的概念：当利润不再是价值增殖的结果，它转化为维持私有制外壳所需的负向政治性维稳成本 P。作者用它描述价值机制枯竭后利润形态的质变。具体推导见全文第三节。

English answer

A concept introduced in the preprint: when profit no longer results from valorization, it becomes transmuted into a negative political cost of maintaining the institutional shell of private property, denoted P. The derivation is in section three of the full text.

什么是商品社会的「四重不可能」？
What is the 'four-fold impossibility' of commodity society?

该预印本用它概括商品社会试图通过自我修复重建价值结构时面临的四重障碍，并据此论证价值结构存在不可逆的内在断裂。四项内容见全文第四节，本站问答只作检索导览，不替代原文。

English answer

The preprint's term for four obstacles commodity society faces when attempting to repair its value structure, used to argue for an irreversible internal rupture. The four items are in section four of the full text; this Q&A is a discovery aid, not a substitute for the paper.
