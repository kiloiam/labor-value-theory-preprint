The Labor Theory of Value as a Historical Category: The Contemporary Crisis of Value

Abstract: Grounding its analysis in historical materialism, this article delineates the historical limits of the labor theory of value (LTV) and analyzes the structural crisis of the law of value under conditions of contemporary automated production. This article maintains that the LTVis not a universal economic principle applicable to all social formations;Rather, it emerges as a historicallyspecific theory, grounded in wage labor, socially necessary labor time, and the universality of commodity exchange. On this basis, a revised value model incorporating automated technological capital is constructed. The paper demonstrates that as automated systems become capable in principleof performing productive functions independently of living labor, variable capital is systematically and irreversibly expelled from the production process.Accordingly, the process of surplus-value production has been tending towards its exhaustion, this process culminates in a fundamental breakdown of the value-category and the commodity-form which depends on it.In this context, profit no longer emerges as the result of valorization. Instead, profit becomes transmutedintoa "negative political cost of social stabilization under private property relations"(P) neededfor the maintenance of the institutional shell (integument) of private property. The article further elaborates on the “four-fold impossibility” of commodity society’s self-repair. In doingso, it exposes an irreversible internal rupture in the value-structure. It concludes that the apparent obsolescence of the LTV does not refute the theory but rather indicates that commoditysociety has reached its historical endpoint. Thus, the central issue in the post-value-category era lies inthe historical reconfiguration of production relations: namely, liberating automated productive capacities from the fetters of private appropriation and reorienting them toward a systemaimedat the socialized organization of use-value. This analysis provides a theoretical criterionfor understanding the specificity of the contemporary capitalist crisis and the direction of future social transformation.

Keywords: Historical Limits of the Labor Theory of Value;Crisis of the Value Category; Automated Production;Historical Restructuring of Production Relations;Class Struggle
